    rect rgb(245,247,248)
    note over U,EXT: INGRESS — establish who is asking, on whose behalf, inside what budget
    U->>EXPR: task arrives on a channel
    EXPR->>GW: session and tenant context
    GW->>GUARD: screen before spending a token
    GUARD-->>GW: cleared · scoped agent identity
    GW->>ORCH: plan, inside this budget
    end

    rect rgb(251,243,234)
    note over U,EXT: AGENT LOOP — plan · ground · gather · reason · gate · act
    loop until the task is done, or the budget or termination policy stops it
        ORCH->>DOM: what do these terms mean?
        DOM-->>ORCH: resolved entities and metrics
        ORCH->>RET: assemble context for this step
        RET->>DATA: governed rows and columns
        DATA-->>RET: data with lineage and entitlements
        RET->>MEM: what do we already know?
        MEM-->>RET: episodic, semantic, procedural
        RET-->>ORCH: context inside the token budget
        ORCH->>GW: reason over this context
        GW->>INFER: routed, cached, metered
        INFER->>CORE: inference on a pinned model
        CORE-->>INFER: completion
        INFER-->>ORCH: next action, schema-valid
        ORCH->>GOV: score against autonomy policy
        alt irreversible, financial or regulated
            GOV->>EXPR: approval at a durable checkpoint
            EXPR-->>GOV: human decision
        else reversible and low impact
            GOV-->>ORCH: proceed unattended, logged
        end
        ORCH->>TOOL: execute the typed tool call
        TOOL->>INFRA: provision an isolated sandbox
        INFRA-->>TOOL: microVM, gVisor or Wasm ready
        opt a classical model answers it better
            TOOL->>MLH: classify, rerank or forecast
            MLH-->>TOOL: score or plan, far cheaper
        end
        TOOL->>PROTO: cross on a standard contract
        PROTO->>EXT: MCP to systems · A2A to peers
        EXT-->>PROTO: result or task handle
        PROTO-->>TOOL: typed result or typed error
        TOOL-->>ORCH: observe, then re-plan
    end
    end

    rect rgb(245,247,248)
    note over U,EXT: COMMIT AND EGRESS — persist, validate, meter, deliver
    ORCH->>MEM: write back what earns persistence
    ORCH->>GUARD: validate the final output
    GUARD-->>GW: schema-valid and policy-clean
    GW->>EXPR: metered and charged to the tenant
    EXPR-->>U: answer, with provenance
    end

    rect rgb(241,229,236)
    note over U,EXT: ASSURANCE AND LIFECYCLE — runs underneath every hop above
    ORCH->>OBS: a span for every hop
    OBS->>EVAL: traces become eval cases
    EVAL->>GOV: evidence for the release gate
    GOV->>ORCH: redeploy versioned artefacts
    GOV->>GUARD: policy compiled into enforcement
    INFRA-->>OBS: capacity, cost and availability
    end
