## Design Context

### Users

Planeon is presented first as an enterprise transformation partner: visitors should understand what changes for their business, what they receive and how to start. The primary action is “Discuss your workflow.” Services, Maturity, Resources and About form the main navigation. Architects and engineers retain the complete blueprint, scenario library and evidence references through Resources and contextual links. Sample deliverables are explicitly illustrative, never invented customer proof.

### Brand Personality

Assured, precise, and composed. The voice is calm and technically authoritative without becoming cold, breathless, or promotional. Planeon should feel like an experienced architecture partner making a complex system legible.

### Aesthetic Direction

A light, editorial technical-document aesthetic aligned with the Planeon brand palette and logo. Soft structural surfaces belong around interactive tools and selected evidence; static reading paths and long-form articles use typography, whitespace and light rules. Retain the floating navigation and asymmetrical reading paths without turning every section into a tray. Keep Poppins headings and self-hosted Geist body text. Use diffused blue-ink ambient shadows, not harsh outlines. Preserve crisp geometric diagrams and plane colors for their semantic roles. Avoid neon effects, decorative charts, competing animations, and marketing claims about implementation maturity.

Light remains the default. Visitors can explicitly select a restrained ink-dark reading mode, retained locally across pages and synchronized across tabs. Dark surfaces use lighter, contrast-tested equivalents of the same four semantic plane hues. Never invert source images or the film; retain the logo symbol and blue .ai pixels, adapting only its dark wordmark letters for legibility. Theme controls must remain keyboard-accessible and available in the mobile navigation.

The homepage introduction uses a restrained blue/teal traveling-wave field: six loose horizontal ribbons, a broad 20-second left-to-right wave and a smaller secondary ripple. Keep the approved density of 168 desktop / 48 mobile dots. Hover gently parts nearby dots, then they settle back into their wave paths without snapping. Keep the business proposition, film and controls in clear exclusion zones. This is decorative, not another architecture diagram: do not extend it to technical workbenches or use the four plane colors as particle categories. Honor reduced motion, saved pause, data-saving and visibility preferences; preserve a static alternative and an accessible pause control.

### Design Principles

1. Make the business offer understandable first. Explain the architecture when the reader asks for depth.
2. Preserve semantic meaning: runtime, knowledge, execution, and trust colors are not decorative accents.
3. Use restrained motion to explain sequence or state, with an equivalent static reduced-motion experience.
4. Maintain technical precision through readable type, stable numbering, aligned columns, and restrained nested framing. Keep working surfaces compact enough to use; macro spacing belongs to editorial sections.
5. Keep vendor-neutral framework content visually distinct from Planeon positioning.
